Diagnostics

How to use this page during detention:

1. Use the Sort Options and Year Filter buttons to show the appropriate list of students.

2. Tick the checkbox next to each student who is physically present at detention.

3. If most students on the page attended, use Select All and then untick any students who are absent.

4. Click Successfully Completed Detention. Selected students are marked served; due students not selected are marked absent from detention.

5. After the roll is marked, use the green Attendance Confirmation Only upload with full-day or weekly absence data to confirm whether missed students were present at school.

6. If you mark a student by mistake, click Undo on that student's row to reverse the most recent served entry.
